Griffies

1. Drag Griffies into Applications.
2. The first time, RIGHT-CLICK Griffies in Applications and choose Open, then Open again.
   (It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, so a normal double-click is blocked.)

If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", run this once in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Griffies.app

Your accounts, settings and AI keys stay on this Mac, in ~/.config/sniffies-messages/.
